# Training guides, by role

> Version 0.1 · 2026-11-12 · G8 (D-118). One section per role, written for society staff and members. Use it in the training sessions (about one hour per role, on staging) and leave a printed copy at each desk. Screen names are as they appear in the console menu and in the phone app.

## For everyone on the staff

**Where you work.** At a desk: the **admin console** in a browser (Chrome or Edge). On the move: the same work in the **TGSECCSL app** on your phone, under *Staff sign-in*. Both show the same records at the same moment.

**Signing in.** User ID and password, then the six-digit code from the authenticator app on your phone (Google or Microsoft Authenticator). The first time, you scan a QR code and choose a new password. Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. Forgot your password or changed phones? Ask the Super Admin to reset it.

**Your day starts at Work Queue.** Everything waiting for you is there, oldest first, with how long it has waited against the society's service target. Open an item, read the record, and use the one button for your step. Every button that cannot be undone asks you to confirm and tells you who acts next.

**Signatures.** Your name (top right) → My signature: draw or upload it once. It is printed on the forms you sign off.

**Finding someone.** The search box (Ctrl K) finds members by name, General No., employee ID or mobile, and loans by number.

**Rules of the house.** Never share your password or authenticator. Sign out on shared PCs. On the phone, the staff screens lock after 5 minutes away. A mistake in money is corrected with a reversal or a new entry, never by deleting; ask the Accountant.

## Accountant

You run the office: intake, checks, the books and month end.

| Task | Where | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| New membership application | Joining | Check documents and details → **Send to DDO**. After the DDO, **Office review** → check duplicates and the bye-law conditions → **Recommend** (or **Return for information** with a clear reason) |
| Loan from the app | Loans → the application | **Review** → (DDO certificate) → **Assessment**: the office-use sheet works out EMI, share deposit and net payable; check it against the rules → send for sanction |
| Loan from a paper form | Loans → New application | Enter F2 / F3 as written; attach the signed paper; the same steps follow |
| FD at the counter | Deposits → Open FD at counter | Enter F4; the Cashier records the money |
| Month end | Demand Sheets | **Generate** the month → open each sheet → **Certify** → **Send to DDO**. When the DDO has entered deductions (or you have the paper statement), **Enter recovery** → check the split → **Post** |
| Members who paid by QR | Payments → Reported by members | The Cashier verifies; you see the receipt |
| Defaulters | Reports → Defaulters list → Defaulter notice | Print and send; recover from sureties after the set number of months (Loans → the loan → Recover from sureties) |
| Office expenses and income | Accounts → New voucher | Choose payment or receipt, the head, cash or bank, amount, narration → **Post** → print |
| Books | Reports → Day book, Cash book, Bank book, General ledger, Trial balance, Profit & loss, Balance sheet | Any date range; CSV for Excel, Print for the file |
| Year end | Accounts → Year end | Interest on deposits → dividend → **Close the year** (after the audit is agreed) |
| Member changes | Members → the member | Approve profile change requests; transfers; a member leaving (Settlement) |
| Messages and questions | Notifications | **Send a message** (preview first); **Member questions** → reply |

## Secretary and President

You approve. Each approval is recorded with your name, time and signature.

| Task | Where | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Membership | Work Queue → application | Office recommendation, documents, DDO certificate → **Approve** or **Reject** with a reason. The President also chooses how the share is paid (counter or first demand) |
| Loan sanction | Work Queue → loan | Assessment sheet (amount, EMI, net payable, sureties' exposure) → **Sanction** or **Send back** with a reason |
| FD acceptance | Work Queue → FD | Payment recorded by the Cashier → **Accept** |
| Overview | Dashboard; Reports | Collections this month, loans outstanding, defaulters, maturities |

On the phone: the Queue tab shows the same list; approve with the same buttons, then confirm with your fingerprint or PIN when asked.

## Cashier

You handle money in and out. Cash always needs the denominations, and the count must equal the amount.

| Task | Where | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Share payment of a new member | Work Queue → application | **Record payment** (cash with denominations, or transfer with reference) → receipt prints |
| FD payment | Deposits → FD requests → the request | **Record payment** → receipt |
| Loan disbursement | Work Queue → loan (sanctioned) | Mode (NEFT / cheque / transfer), reference, date, payee if someone else → **Disburse**; the loan receipt (F7) prints with your PAID signature; the borrower confirms in the app |
| Counter receipt | Payments → New receipt | Member → heads (dues are filled in) → cash / transfer → **Save** → print |
| Member-reported payment | Payments → Reported by members | Find the credit in the bank statement → tick → **Verify & create receipt**; if not found, **Reject** with the reason |
| FD payouts and withdrawal | Deposits → the FD | Maturity payout, early withdrawal (penalty as set) → voucher prints |

## DDO (Drawing & Disbursing Officer)

You see only your department.

| Task | Where | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Certify a new member | Work Queue → application | Check items 1–7 (service, pay, deductions) → **Certify** and agree the salary recovery mandate |
| Certify a loan | Work Queue → loan | Items 1–5 (pay and deductions, recovery feasible) → **Certify** |
| Monthly deductions | Demand Sheets → this month | Enter what was deducted for each member (or "in full") → **Submit**. Members short of the demand are flagged to the society |

## Super Admin

You look after users, settings and the audit.

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Add, change, deactivate staff; reset password or authenticator | Admin → Users |
| Who may do what | Settings → Permissions (a change is recorded) |
| Society profile, share value, limits, service targets, holidays, caste categories, oldest app version, personal-data retention | Settings → General |
| Loan products (rates, limits, sureties, EMI method) | Settings → Loan products (each change is a new version) |
| FD slabs, penalties, CRD / SRDS | Settings → Deposits |
| Message templates | Settings → Templates |
| Who did what, when | Audit Log |

Settings marked **placeholder** wait for the society's decision; change them only on a committee decision and note it.

## Members (handout)

**Get the app:** "TGSECCSL" on Google Play or the App Store. **Sign in:** your mobile number registered with the society, then the code sent to your WhatsApp. No password.

| You want to | In the app |
|---|---|
| See your shares, loans, deposits and dues | Home |
| Apply for a loan | Loans → Apply (choose sureties; they agree in their own app) |
| Agree to be someone's surety | Home → Waiting for you |
| Open a fixed deposit | Deposits → Open FD; pay at the counter or by the society's QR code |
| See how your salary deduction was used | Payments |
| Tell the office you paid by QR / UPI | Payments → Report a payment (date and UTR) |
| Change your address or nominee | Profile → Request a change |
| Ask the office something | More → Help → Ask a question |
| Choose WhatsApp or email messages | More → Settings |
| Read the privacy notice, or ask to delete your app account | More → Settings → Account |

Never share the code you receive. The society never asks for it on the phone.

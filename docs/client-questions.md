# Questions for the society (TGSECCSL)

> Version 1.0 · 2026-10-25 · For the society's office bearers. Plain language, no system terms.
> Each question shows **what the demo does today**. Every one of these is a setting the Super Admin can change in a minute (Settings), so nothing has to be rebuilt once you answer.
> Technical tracking: `docs/open-questions.md` (the Q-numbers in brackets).

## 1. Membership

1. **Is the "General Number" your society membership number, or the government employee number?** (Q-501)
   *Today:* the society's own number. It is shown on every screen and form.
2. **How many shares must a new member buy, what is one share worth, and is there an admission fee?** Please also share the back side of the membership form. (Q-506)
   *Today:* a share is ₹100, 10 to 100 shares, no admission fee.
3. **Is a new member approved by the Secretary then the President, or by a committee resolution?** (Q-511)
   *Today:* Secretary, then President.
4. **If the share money is cut from salary, is the person a member from approval, or from the first deduction?** (Q-510)
   *Today:* a member from approval. The share money appears on the next month's salary-deduction list.
5. **May the forms use the society's current name, and is signing with a one-time code (OTP) on the phone legally acceptable?** Or do you need a pen signature or Aadhaar eSign? (Q-505)
   *Today:* current name, signing with an OTP.
6. **Which documents must a new member upload?** (Q-403)
   *Today:* four sample document types.
7. **Why is caste recorded, and who may see it?** (Q-406)
   *Today:* only the Super Admin and the Accountant.

## 2. Loans

8. **For each loan type, what are the interest rate, the largest amount and the longest repayment period?** (Q-311)
   *Today:*
   - Ordinary: 11%, up to ₹5,00,000, 100 months;
   - Education: 9%, up to ₹3,00,000, 60 months;
   - Salary: 12%, up to ₹1,00,000, 24 months;
   - Loan against deposit: 10%, up to 90% of the deposit, 36 months.
9. **How is the monthly instalment worked out?** The same amount every month, or a fixed part of the loan plus interest on what is still owed? (Q-302)
   *Today:* a fixed part of the loan plus interest on what is still owed.
10. **Salary loan: how much can a member take, and is it repaid in one cut from the next salary or in instalments?** Confirm that no sureties and no DDO certificate are needed. (Q-502)
    *Today:* up to the pay earned so far, no sureties, no DDO certificate.
11. **Is the 10% share deduction for Ordinary loans also taken on other loan types?** (Q-304)
    *Today:* Ordinary loans only.
12. **Can a member hold more than one loan of the same type?** (Q-115)
    *Today:* one per type, except loans against deposits (two).
13. **Is there a limit on how much of the take-home pay instalments may use?** (Q-503)
    *Today:* the ratio is shown to staff and warns above 50%, but never blocks.
14. **What happens to a loan when a member retires, resigns or dies?** (Q-315)
    *Today:* the repayment period cannot go past the retirement date. Settlement after leaving is not in the demo.
15. **What can be offered as security besides sureties?** (Q-507)
    *Today:* sureties, a pledged fixed deposit, or "other" (described in words).

## 3. Sureties

16. **How many loans may one member guarantee at a time?** And if a surety retires before the loan ends, is that only a warning, or are they not allowed? (Q-504)
    *Today:* two loans at a time. Retiring early only shows a warning.

## 4. Deposits and savings

17. **What do CRD, SRDS and RRF stand for, and how much is collected each month?** (Q-301)
    *Today:* CRD ₹500, SRDS ₹300 and RRF ₹100 a month. CRD earns 6% and SRDS 6.5%.
18. **Fixed deposits: the interest for each period, the smallest amount, how interest is added, and the rules for closing early.** (Q-313)
    *Today:*
    - 1–2 years 7%, 2–3 years 7.25%, 3–5 years 7.5%;
    - the smallest FD is ₹10,000;
    - early closure is not in the demo.
19. **How do savings deposits and withdrawals happen, and what interest do they earn?** (Q-312)
    *Today:* view and statement only, at 4%.

## 5. Monthly salary deductions (the demand sheet)

20. **On which day of the month is the deduction list made, and what is the cut-off for new loans and transfers?** (Q-308)
    *Today:* monthly. A transfer counts from the next list that has not been made yet.
21. **When a salary deduction comes short, in what order is the money used?** And what happens to any extra? (Q-309)
    *Today:* the fixed order on your recovery agreement. Anything extra stays with the member as a credit.
22. **Are unpaid amounts carried into next month's list?** (Q-316)
    *Today:* yes, as separate lines.
23. **Insurance: how much, once a year or monthly, and in which month?** (Q-306)
    *Today:* once a year in April. ₹1,000 for members with a loan, ₹200 for others.
24. **Are there late-payment charges?** (Q-307)
    *Today:* none.
25. **When is a member a "defaulter", and what should the defaulter notice say?** (Q-310)
    *Today:* after 3 months in a row unpaid. The notice wording can be edited in Settings → Templates.

## 6. Staff and how the office works

26. **Is there one DDO per department, or more than one?** (Q-101)
    *Today:* one per department.
27. **Is the Cashier the same person who pays out loans?** Should notes be counted for every cash receipt? (Q-509)

28. **How do staff and members sign in?** (Q-103)
    *Today:*
    - staff: user ID and password;
    - members: General No. or Employee ID with a password;
    - first-time activation and forgotten passwords use a one-time code.
29. **Which events should send a message by SMS, WhatsApp or email, and to whom?** (Q-111)
    *Today:* messages are simulated and shown inside the apps. Their wording is in Settings → Templates.
30. **Which bank account or UPI details should members see when they report a payment?** Is a screenshot required? (Q-120)
    *Today:* sample account details. The reference number is required; the screenshot is optional.
31. **Members transferred outside the Secretariat (deputation): how are deductions collected?** (Q-512)
    *Today:* not in the demo.
